Review this automated assessment engine for correctness,
maintainability, scalability, privacy, and testability.

Focus on:
1. incorrect score calculations,
2. missing boundary conditions,
3. excessive cyclomatic complexity,
4. duplicated grading rules,
5. weak exception handling,
6. nondeterministic behavior,
7. sensitive student data in logs,
8. missing auditability,
9. concurrency or idempotency risks,
10. modularity and extensibility.

Do not rewrite the entire module.
For every finding, provide:
- severity,
- file,
- function,
- explanation,
- reproduction scenario,
- proposed correction,
- verification test.

Clearly state uncertainty.
